6. Summary and Research Implications

  • If crash coefficients are negative and significant: safety skims are behaviourally valid — model it.
  • If Δρ² > 0.002: meaningful improvement in destination choice predictive power.
  • If active travel share drops with safety skims: crash penalisation is selecting safer but lower-speed routes, as hypothesised.
  • If LRT p < 0.01 across all segments: strong evidence for inclusion in the final published model.
  • Zone derivation: zones with high OSM walkability/cycling features and large negative Δ active travel are the zones where the safety skim is actively suppressing behaviour — these are the zones to highlight in the paper.
